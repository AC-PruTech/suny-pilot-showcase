SUNY Pilot · Build Showcase

One pilot, every prototype in one place.

Browse the learner community, run the rules engine, and check the backend record — a connected walkthrough of the SUNY Pilot continuing-education build, from discovery and checkout to employer-sponsored payment.

Fictional sample — not official SUNY. SUNY campus names are public; every learner, course, price, seat, and record on these pages is invented pilot sample data. Nothing here connects to a Salesforce org, submits, enrols, or charges anyone.

Single custom LWR community: the learner experience is designed as one Experience Cloud site on a custom LWR template — 64 campuses handled by campus filtering and audience targeting, with guest prospects and authenticated learners in the same site. LWC-only, SLDS 2. It is not an LMS.

The prototypes

Everything below is a static, self-contained prototype in this folder — no build step, no server-side code, no org connection.

1 · Learner experience

Community Prototype

The SUNY Pilot learner community: Discover across 64 campuses, non-credit / CE-first course catalogue (plus for-credit context), learner home, programs, payments, support — and a simulated checkout that charges nothing.

  • Discover & campus filtering (64 SUNY campuses)
  • Non-credit CE detail, CEUs, flat-fee pricing
  • Checkout, learner home & support views

Older framed wrapper: community.html (legacy Hartwell-titled viewer around the same community — superseded by this showcase; kept for reference).

2 · Staff experience

Backend Lightning Console

An Education Cloud–style Lightning console mirror: workspace queues, Learner 360, Application, Program Enrollment, Case, Product2 (CE course), Financial Aid Award, PSS State Payment, and Order records.

  • Standard-first objects & record pages
  • PSS payment by record type (Employer_Sponsored / Degree_Financial_Aid / State_Workforce_Funding)
Open backend-lightning.html →

3 · Decision layer

BRE Rules Engine

The Business Rules Engine tab inside the backend console: the SUNY_CE_Eligibility rule-set showcase, sample decision tables, and an in-page decision checker whose result is (in-org, conceptually) stored on the Order as decision + reason + version.

  • Expression Sets + Decision Tables (concept)
  • Confirm BRE licence / API in-org
Open backend-lightning.html#bre →

4 · Interactive sandbox

Interactive BRE Mock (from Edu Atlas)

A hands-on rules sandbox patterned on the Edu Atlas BRE mock and remapped to SUNY rule sets: employer sponsorship splits, funding stacks, refunds, pricing, go/no-go cohorts, Workforce Pell checks, and more. Edit the decision tables and re-run.

  • Opens on the employer-sponsored seat scenario
  • Shows the Salesforce / PSS routing for each result
Open bre-mock.html →
