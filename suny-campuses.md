## Verification & discrepancy

No discrepancy in the final count on https://www.suny.edu/campuses/ when Cornell's four statutory colleges are counted individually: 14 doctoral + 13 university colleges + 7 technology colleges + 30 community colleges = 64. A related page, https://www.suny.edu/contact/campus-directories/, groups the four Cornell colleges under one heading 'NYS Colleges at Cornell University', so a literal heading count there is 61 headings, which expands to 64 campuses. The SUNY Plattsburgh branch at Queensbury listed in the Dec 2022 Authorized Names PDF is a branch, not counted as a separate 65th campus. Direct curl to suny.edu from the box returned Cloudflare 403 'Just a moment'; pages above were successfully fetched via WebFetch on 2026-10-18 and are cited, not fabricated.

Sector counts: Community College: 30 · University Center / Doctoral Degree Granting Institution: 14 · Technology College: 7 · University College: 13 = **64**.
